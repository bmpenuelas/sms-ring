Lost your phone between the sofa cushions, on silent? Text it a secret phrase from any phone and it rings at full volume, even on silent. Online phone finders can only ring a phone that’s connected to the internet. SMS Ring just needs mobile signal.

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN, NOT BY PROMISE
SMS Ring has no internet permission. The operating system itself blocks every connection, so your data physically cannot leave your phone. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no servers.

👀 OPEN SOURCE: VERIFY IT YOURSELF
Every line of code is public. Read it, build it, check it.

✨ FEATURES
🔔 Full volume, even on silent or vibrate
🎲 Random passphrase of 2 to 6 easy words in your language, or write your own
📱 Allow anyone, or only the numbers you choose
💬 Android: also works with WhatsApp, Messenger, Telegram, Signal or any other app if you allow notification access. It stays on your phone, fully private.
⏱️ Ring for 15 seconds up to 5 minutes
📳 Optional vibration
🌙 Rings through Do Not Disturb if you allow it
🔓 Only you can stop it: stopping asks for your PIN or fingerprint
🧪 Test ring and a message simulator, all offline
🌍 Available in 20 languages

iPhone: Apple doesn’t let apps read messages. SMS Ring guides you through a 1-minute Shortcuts automation that wakes it when your passphrase arrives.
