Make your phone ring at full volume by sending an SMS to it. It rings at full volume, even on silent. No internet connection is needed, works even with just a weak mobile signal.

Online phone finders can only ring a phone that’s connected to the internet, and they require you to log in on another device, connect to your phone... SMS Ring just needs mobile signal. No internet, no accounts, no logins.

► Full volume, even on silent or vibrate
► Optional vibration, and vibration-only mode
► Choose your secret passphrase to make it ring
► Allow from any number, or only the numbers you choose
► Only you can stop it: stopping asks for your PIN or fingerprint

► Private by design, not by promise
SMS Ring has no internet permission. The operating system itself blocks every connection, so your data physically cannot leave your phone. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no servers.

SETUP (IMPORTANT!): Apple doesn’t let apps read messages. SMS Ring guides you through a 1-minute Shortcuts automation that you have to create for the app to be able to read the incoming message. Every text that should ring your phone must also contain the word “ring”. SMS Ring adds it when you copy or share your message.
