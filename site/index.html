Lost your phone on silent? Text it.

Send it a secret phrase and it rings at full volume. No accounts, no cloud, no internet. Private by design, open source so you can check every line.

Works even when your phone is offline. Find My and similar services can only ring a phone with mobile data or Wi-Fi on. SMS Ring just needs mobile signal.

Why you'll love it

Everything it takes to find a phone that's buried in a sofa, and nothing more.

  • 🔊

    Rings loud, even on silent

    Full volume through silent mode, vibrate mode and Do Not Disturb.

  • 🎲

    A secret phrase that's easy to say

    2 to 6 random everyday words like “daisy meadow rocket”, in your own language. Prefer your own? You can create your own password.

  • 💬

    Works with chat apps too

    On Android, WhatsApp, Messenger, Telegram, Signal or any other app can ring it, if you turn on notification access. Notifications are checked only on your phone and never stored or sent: it stays fully private.

  • 👨‍👩‍👧

    You decide who can ring it

    Anyone who knows the phrase, or only the numbers you add.

  • ⏱️

    Your ring, your rules

    Ring for 15 seconds up to 5 minutes, at the volume you choose (full by default), with or without vibration, with one of several tones (or your own alarm sound on Android). Light or dark look, or follow your phone.

  • 🛡️

    Ready when it matters

    Rings with the screen off, while the phone sleeps, and after a restart (on Android even before you unlock it). A daily check tells you if something it needs was turned off.

  • 🧪

    Try before you need it

    A test ring and a message simulator show exactly what will happen, without sending anything.

  • 🔓

    Only you can stop it

    A big Stop button appears over the lock screen, like an alarm clock. Stopping asks for your PIN, pattern or fingerprint, so whoever finds your phone can't silence it.

  • 🎯

    Exact on purpose

    Only the exact phrase rings, so no everyday conversation can set it off. Capitals, accents and spaces don't matter: “Daisy MEADOW rocket” works.

  • 🤝

    Share it with family

    Send your phrase to someone you trust in one tap, so they can help you find your phone.

  • 🌍

    Speaks your language

    20 languages, from English and Español to 日本語, العربية and हिन्दी.

  • ⚡

    Ready in a few taps

    The phrase is generated for you and it's on from the start. Setup is just a few permission taps, each explained first.

Privacy guaranteed by design, not by promise

SMS Ring can't send your data anywhere, because it has no way to connect to anything.

  • 🚫🌐No internet permission at all

    Android itself blocks every connection the app could try to make. See it in Settings → Apps → SMS Ring → Permissions, and the app shows you a live check.

  • 🧠Messages are never stored

    Each text is checked in memory for your phrase and forgotten immediately.

  • 🙅No accounts, ads, analytics or servers

    There is nothing to sign up for and nowhere for data to go.

  • 👀Open source

    Read the code, build it yourself, verify everything. Run npm run verify:offline to check the claims automatically.
